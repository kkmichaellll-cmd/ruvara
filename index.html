About Ruvara (roo-VAH-rah)

Creating unique nails, one set at a time.

At Ruvara (roo-VAH-rah), colour is at the heart of everything we do. Inspired by the Shona word for "colour," our name reflects our belief that every shade tells a story. We create thoughtfully designed nails that celebrate your individuality and reflect your personal style.

Your natural nail health is always our priority. We use products that meet EU and Dutch safety standards; we deliver beautiful, long-lasting results without compromising the health of your nails.

By working by appointment only, we provide a personalised experience in a clean, welcoming space where every detail is focused on you…

Questions & Answers

Common Curations

Where are you located?

I am located in Arnhem Zuid. The exact address will be shared after your appointment is confirmed.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book by via my online booking system.

What are your opening hours?

I am available by appointment only. Please check the booking platform for availability.

Do you offer home services?

Yes, I offer home services at an additional fee.

How long will my appointment take?

This depends on the service, but most appointments take between 1–2.5 hours.

What payment methods do you accept, and is a deposit required?

Cash or Tikkie are accepted. A 15 euro non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment, with the remaining balance due after your treatment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, if not you risk losing your deposit.

What products do you use?

I use professional-quality products that comply with Dutch and EU cosmetic regulations, ensuring safety and quality standards.

How do you sterilise your tools?

All reusable tools are cleaned and disinfected between every client. Single-use items are replaced after each appointment.
